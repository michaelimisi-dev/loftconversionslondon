Loft rooms

Loft Bathroom & En-Suite Conversion in London

A loft bathroom or en-suite conversion in London turns a new loft bedroom into a self-contained suite, which is exactly what buyers look for. We plan the drainage, ventilation and waterproofing into the structural design, so the bathroom works properly under the roof from day one.

  • Shower rooms, en-suites and full bathrooms
  • Drainage route planned at the free survey
  • Proper extraction and waterproof tanking
  • Part of a new loft, or added to an existing one

What's included

What a Loft Bathroom Conversion Includes

  • Layout design that puts the shower, WC and basin where the head height is.
  • Drainage: connection to the existing soil stack where possible, or a new stack.
  • Hot water and heating: a check that your boiler or cylinder can supply a top-floor shower, and a heated towel rail.
  • Ventilation: an extractor ducted outside, meeting Approved Document F.
  • Waterproofing: tanking behind tiles and around the shower tray, and floor protection.
  • Electrics: bathroom-zone lighting and shaver point, installed and certified under Part P.
  • Tiling and sanitaryware fitted to your chosen specification.

Is it right for you?

Signs You Should Add a Bathroom to Your Loft

  • The new loft bedroom will be the main bedroom, and you want an en-suite.
  • Mornings are a queue for the only bathroom in the house.
  • You plan to sell within a few years and want the best possible value.
  • Your existing loft bedroom means a trip downstairs at night.
  • Your house has a back addition where a loft pod could hold a shower room.

Our process

How We Plan and Build a Loft Bathroom

  1. Survey: locate the soil stack, check the boiler or cylinder, measure head heights.
  2. Layout: place fittings where the height and drainage work, and agree the specification.
  3. First fix: waste pipes, water supply and electrics run before the walls are boarded.
  4. Waterproofing: moisture-resistant boards and tanking in all wet areas.
  5. Second fix: tiling, sanitaryware, extractor, lighting and towel rail.
  6. Testing and sign-off: drainage and electrics tested, then building control inspection.

Pricing

What Affects the Cost of a Loft Bathroom in London?

  • Distance to the soil stack. This makes the biggest difference, and is why we decide the layout at the survey.
  • Shower room or full bathroom: a bath needs more space and a stronger floor.
  • Hot water: a combi boiler may struggle with a top-floor shower; an unvented cylinder may be needed.
  • Tiles and fittings: specification is the easiest place to control the budget.
  • Building it with the loft: adding a bathroom during the conversion is far cheaper than retrofitting later.

Local knowledge

Loft Bathrooms in London Homes

In most London terraces, the original bathroom is at the back of the first floor, often in the back addition, with the soil stack on the rear wall. That makes the rear of the loft the natural place for a new bathroom, directly above the existing one. In London's many conservation areas, councils often restrict new pipework on front elevations, which is another reason to keep the bathroom at the back.

15 l/s

Ventilation That Protects Your Roof

Steam in a loft condenses on cold roof timbers if it is not removed. Approved Document F requires at least 15 litres per second of extraction in a bathroom. We fit extractors ducted to outside, never into the roof void, and insulate the ducting to stop drips.

Part P

Certified Bathroom Electrics

Electrical work in a bathroom is notifiable under Part P of the Building Regulations. Every circuit in your loft bathroom is installed by a qualified electrician, tested and certified, so you have the paperwork when you sell.
