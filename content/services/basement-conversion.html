Conversions

Basement Conversion in London

A basement conversion in London turns a damp, unused cellar into dry, warm living space: a family room, home office, gym or guest bedroom. When the roof is not an option, or you have already converted the loft, the space under your house is the next place to look.

  • Existing cellars converted into habitable rooms
  • Waterproofing designed to BS 8102
  • Often no planning permission needed
  • Ventilation, insulation and escape routes included

What's included

What a Basement Conversion Includes

  • Survey: head height, signs of damp, the condition of walls and floor, and the drainage position.
  • Waterproofing design to BS 8102, usually a cavity drainage membrane with a sump and pump.
  • Floor: a new insulated floor, sometimes lowered to gain head height.
  • Structural work where walls need underpinning or openings need steel beams.
  • Ventilation and daylight: mechanical ventilation and, where planning allows, a lightwell.
  • Fire safety: an escape window or protected route, and linked smoke alarms.
  • Fit-out and Building Regulations sign-off for a change to habitable use.

Is it right for you?

Signs Your Cellar Is Ready for Conversion

  • Your house has an existing cellar you can walk into, even if it is low or damp.
  • You have already converted the loft, or the roof is unsuitable.
  • You need a room away from the bedrooms: a gym, cinema room or office.
  • Your street is in a conservation area where roof extensions are refused.
  • Houses nearby have already converted their cellars.

Our process

How a Basement Conversion Is Done

  1. Free survey, including damp readings and head height.
  2. Waterproofing and structural design, plus planning and party wall checks.
  3. Strip out of old finishes, debris and any rotten timber.
  4. Floor lowering or underpinning, where needed for head height.
  5. Waterproofing: membrane, drainage channels, sump and pump.
  6. Insulation, services and fit-out.
  7. Building control sign-off as a habitable room.

Pricing

How Much Does a Basement Conversion Cost in London?

MyBuilder puts a London cellar conversion at around £45,000 to £75,000, and Homebuilding advises allowing about £1,350 to £1,650 per square metre plus VAT for a straightforward cellar conversion.1, 2 The main factors are:

  • Head height: lowering the floor and underpinning walls adds significant cost.
  • Water: the more water present, the more robust the waterproofing system needed.
  • Lightwells: they bring in daylight and an escape route but need excavation and planning.
  • Access: removing spoil through a terraced house is slower than through a side entrance.
  • Fit-out: a shower room, kitchenette or cinema setup adds to the base cost.

Local knowledge

Basement Conversions on London Clay

Much of London sits on London Clay, which holds water and swells and shrinks with the seasons. That is why waterproofing matters more here than almost anywhere else, and why most London basements use a drained cavity membrane that copes with movement. Many Victorian houses in inner boroughs were built with coal cellars or vaults under the front path. Several inner London councils have policies limiting how far new basements can extend under gardens, so we check yours before design starts.
