L-Shaped Dormer Loft Conversion in London

An L-shaped dormer loft conversion in London is built for the classic Victorian or Edwardian terrace with a back addition. One dormer goes on the main roof and a second over the outrigger, joined into an L, giving you space for two bedrooms, or a bedroom with its own bathroom.

  • Made for London terraces with a back addition
  • Room for two bedrooms or a bedroom and bathroom
  • Often achievable under permitted development
  • Party wall notices prepared for you

What's included

What an L-Shaped Dormer Conversion Includes

  • Survey and design of both roofs, the outrigger walls and the stair position.
  • Volume calculation against the 40 cubic metre permitted development limit for terraced houses.
  • Structural engineering for the steels, floor joists and the junction where the two dormers meet.
  • Approvals: lawful development certificate or planning application, Building Regulations approval and party wall notices to both neighbours.
  • Both dormers built, roofed, insulated and finished externally to suit your house.
  • Fit-out: floor, insulation, services, plastering, staircase and fire-safe escape route.
  • Sign-off with building control's completion certificate.

Is it right for you?

Signs Your House Suits an L-Shaped Dormer

  • Your house is a Victorian or Edwardian terrace with a narrower two-storey wing at the back.
  • The back addition has its own pitched roof, lower than the main roof.
  • You want two rooms in the loft, not one.
  • A single rear dormer would leave you without space for a bathroom.
  • Neighbours on your street have already built L-shaped dormers. That is a good sign your council accepts them.

Our process

How an L-Shaped Dormer Is Built

  1. Free survey of the main roof, outrigger roof and party walls.
  2. Design and volume check, adjusting the dormer sizes if needed to stay within permitted development.
  3. Approvals and party wall notices, served at least two months before work on the shared walls.
  4. Scaffold over the rear of the house and the back addition.
  5. Steels and floor installed, then both dormer frames built and joined.
  6. Roofing, windows and external finish.
  7. Fit-out, staircase and final inspection.

Pricing

What Affects the Cost of an L-Shaped Dormer in London?

MyJobQuote puts London L-shaped dormers at roughly £1,900 to £2,300 per square metre.1 Within that range, cost depends on:

  • Size of both dormers and how much of the outrigger roof is converted.
  • Party wall surveyor fees, which can apply on both sides of a mid-terrace.
  • Bathroom position: placing it over the back addition is often close to the existing soil pipe, which keeps plumbing simpler.
  • External finish: tile hanging, slate or render to match neighbouring conversions.
  • Windows and glazing, including any Juliet balcony doors.

Local knowledge

Why L-Shaped Dormers Are So Common in London

Hundreds of thousands of London terraces were built between the 1850s and 1910s with a two-storey back addition housing the kitchen and a bedroom or bathroom above. That layout is everywhere from Walthamstow and Leyton to Lambeth, Lewisham and Wandsworth, and it is exactly what an L-shaped dormer is designed for. The Planning Portal confirms that roof work joining the roof of a rear extension is exempt from the 20 cm eaves set-back, which is why the outrigger dormer can run right to its edge.3

Why Choose Us for Your L-Shaped Dormer

Designed to Fit Permitted Development

L-shaped dormers on terraces can come close to the 40 cubic metre limit. We calculate the combined volume of both dormers at the design stage and size them to stay within it where possible. That avoids a planning application, and we apply for a lawful development certificate to prove it.

2 neighbours

Party Walls on Both Sides

On a mid-terrace, both neighbours usually need notice under the Party Wall etc. Act 1996, at least two months before work on the shared walls. We help you serve clear, correct notices early, which is the best way to keep neighbours on side and avoid surveyor disputes.

2 rooms

Layouts That Use Every Metre

The junction between the two dormers is where an L-shaped loft is won or lost. We plan the staircase landing, bedroom and bathroom around it at the survey, so you get two usable rooms rather than one good room and an awkward corridor.
