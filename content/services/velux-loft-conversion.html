Loft conversions

Velux Loft Conversion in London

A Velux loft conversion in London is the simplest way to use your roof space when it already has enough height. The roof keeps its shape, and rooflight windows are fitted into the slope to fill the new room with daylight. With less structural work, it is the quickest and lowest-cost type of loft conversion.

  • Roof shape stays exactly as it is
  • Usually no planning permission needed
  • Lowest cost of any loft conversion
  • Plenty of daylight from roof windows

What's included

What a Velux Loft Conversion Includes

  • Survey to confirm the head height and check the roof timbers.
  • Structural design for new floor joists strong enough for a habitable room, plus any trimming around the rooflights.
  • Rooflight windows cut into the roof slope, flashed and weatherproofed, usually with blinds.
  • Insulation between and under the rafters to modern Building Regulations standards.
  • Floor, walls and ceiling: new flooring, plasterboard, skirting and decoration-ready finish.
  • Staircase, electrics and heating, plus fire doors and linked smoke alarms for a safe escape route.
  • Building Regulations approval and a completion certificate. A rooflight conversion still needs one.

Is it right for you?

Signs a Velux Loft Conversion Will Work in Your Home

  • You can stand up comfortably in the middle of the loft, with roughly 2.2 metres under the ridge.
  • The roof is steeply pitched, as on many Victorian and Edwardian houses.
  • You want a bedroom, office or playroom without changing how the house looks.
  • Your house is in a conservation area where dormers are hard to get approved.
  • You want to keep the budget and the disruption as low as possible.

If the loft is too low, a dormer loft conversion or a mansard can create the extra height.

Our process

How a Velux Loft Conversion Is Built

  1. Free survey with head height measurements at several points.
  2. Design and Building Regulations application, including the staircase and escape route.
  3. Floor structure: new joists laid alongside the existing ceiling joists.
  4. Rooflights: openings cut, trimmed and the windows fitted and flashed.
  5. Insulation and boarding of the roof slopes and walls.
  6. Staircase, services and finishes.
  7. Final inspection and completion certificate.

Pricing

How Much Does a Velux Loft Conversion Cost in London?

MyJobQuote estimates a 30 m² Velux conversion in London at about £23,000 to £34,500.1 The main cost factors are:

  • Number and size of rooflights.
  • Floor structure: the existing ceiling joists are rarely strong enough, so new joists or steels are usually needed.
  • Staircase: the biggest design challenge in a low loft; a space-saving stair may be needed.
  • Insulation: fitting enough insulation without losing headroom sometimes needs high-performance boards.
  • Extras: an en-suite, built-in storage in the eaves, or electric blinds.

Local knowledge

Velux Loft Conversions in London's Conservation Areas

Much of inner London is covered by conservation areas, where roof extensions such as dormers are not permitted development. A rooflight conversion is often the most practical option there because it leaves the roof profile untouched. Under permitted development a rooflight must not project more than 150 mm beyond the roof slope or rise above the ridge.2 Some councils also expect flush conservation-style rooflights on front slopes, so we check the rules for your street.
