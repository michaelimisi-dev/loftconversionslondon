What's included

What a Loft Kitchen Conversion Includes

  • Layout design that keeps the hob away from the escape route and makes the most of the head height.
  • Floor strengthening for the weight of units, worktops and appliances.
  • Water and drainage: supply and waste for the sink and dishwasher, connected to the existing stack.
  • Extraction ducted outside to Approved Document F standards.
  • Electrics: a dedicated cooker circuit and sockets, tested and certified.
  • Fire safety: fire doors, heat and smoke detection, and a safe escape route.
  • Fitting of units, worktops and appliances to your chosen specification.

Is it right for you?

When a Loft Kitchen Makes Sense

  • You own a top-floor flat or maisonette and want to move the living space up into the loft for better light and views.
  • You want an annexe-style suite in your loft for a parent, adult child or au pair.
  • You work from a loft office and want a tea point or kitchenette up there.
  • You are converting a mansion block flat and need the layout to work across two levels.

Pricing

What Affects the Cost of a Loft Kitchen in London?

  • The loft conversion itself: the kitchen is usually part of a larger dormer or mansard project.
  • Distance to drainage and whether a new soil or waste pipe is needed.
  • Extraction route: ducting out through a dormer wall is simpler than through the roof.
  • Kitchen specification: units, worktops and appliances vary enormously in price.
  • Consents for flats: freeholder fees and any premium for the loft space can be significant.

Local knowledge

Loft Kitchens in London Flats and Maisonettes

London has a very high share of converted houses and purpose-built flats, and the owners of top-floor flats often have the best case for a loft kitchen. Moving the kitchen and living room up into the loft frees the floor below for bedrooms. The Planning Portal is clear that flats and maisonettes have no permitted development rights for loft conversions, so planning permission is needed, along with the freeholder's consent.2

Extraction Designed In, Not Added On
