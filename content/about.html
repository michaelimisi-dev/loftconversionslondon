Our approach

How We Work

A loft conversion is one of the biggest projects most people take on in their home. It involves designers, structural engineers, building control, sometimes the council's planning team, and your neighbours. Our job is to bring all of that together so you deal with one team from first survey to final certificate.

We start every project the same way: a free survey where we measure the loft, check the roof structure and head height, and talk through how you want to use the space. Then we tell you plainly which type of conversion suits your house, whether it is likely to be permitted development or need planning permission, and what a realistic budget looks like.

What we stand for

Three Things We Promise Every London Homeowner

1

Honest Prices

We publish the London price ranges from independent guides on our website, because we think you should know roughly what to expect before anyone visits. After the survey you get a fixed written quote that lists exactly what is included.

2

Done Properly, and Signed Off

Every loft is designed to the Building Regulations from the first drawing and finishes with a completion certificate from building control. That certificate is what your buyer's solicitor will ask for when you sell.

3

Good Neighbours

London homes are close together. We help you serve party wall notices correctly and early, keep the site tidy, and respect working hours, so you stay on good terms with the people next door.
