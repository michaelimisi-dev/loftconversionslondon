Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Who we are

This website is operated by Loft Conversions London ("we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal information collected through it under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. You can contact us about privacy at info@loftconversionslondon.co.uk.

2. What we collect

  • Quote and survey requests: your name, phone number, postcode and the type of conversion you are interested in, when you fill in a form.
  • Emails: anything you send us by email.
  • Website usage: pages visited, device and browser type and approximate location, collected by Google Analytics using cookies.

3. How we use it

We use your contact details to respond to your enquiry, arrange a survey and send you a quote. Our lawful basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract. We use analytics data to understand how the website is used and improve it, based on your consent to analytics cookies. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Who we share it with

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which delivers form submissions to our email inbox.
  • Google, which provides Google Analytics.
  • Our email provider, which stores our correspondence.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they rely on safeguards recognised under UK data protection law.

5. Cookies

This website uses Google Analytics cookies to measure visits. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time, and the website will still work.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for up to two years after our last contact with you, unless you become a customer, in which case we keep project records for as long as needed for guarantees, tax and legal purposes.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to withdraw consent at any time. To make a request, email info@loftconversionslondon.co.uk. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
